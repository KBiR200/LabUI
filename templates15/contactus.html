Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new task?

To create a new task, navigate to the Tasks page and click on the "Create Task" button in the top right corner. Fill out the required information in the form that appears and click "Create Task" to save it.

Can I assign multiple team members to a single task?

Currently, each task can only be assigned to one team member at a time. However, you can create subtasks within a main task and assign those to different team members.

How do I generate reports for my team's performance?

You can generate reports by navigating to the Reports page. From there, you can select various filters such as date range, team, and task type to customize your report. Once you've set your filters, click on "Generate Report" to view the results.

Is there a mobile app available?

Yes, we have mobile apps available for both iOS and Android devices. You can download them from the respective app stores by searching for "LabUI". The mobile app provides most of the functionality available in the web version.

How can I reset my password?

If you've forgotten your password, click on the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in page. Enter your email address, and we'll send you instructions on how to reset your password.
